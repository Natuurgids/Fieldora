# Fieldora Navigation

Fieldora separates research work from platform administration.

## Science Workspace

- **Research:** Projects & Tasks, Observation Register, Dossiers, and Research
  Calendar.
- **Library:** Media, Research Documents, Whiteboards, Collections, Notebook,
  and Maps.
- **Scientific Records:** Animals, Plants & Fungi, Other Specimens & Artifacts,
  Life Lists & Statistics, and Habitats & Seasonality.
- **Knowledge:** Knowledge Explorer and Regional Knowledge.
- **Analysis:** Analysis & AI Review, Reports, and Offline Scientific Maps.

## Platform Management

- **People & Governance:** users, roles, permissions, and access contracts.
- **AI & Processing:** models, AI configuration, processing components, and
  enrichment sources.
- **Knowledge Configuration:** knowledge and taxonomy sources.
- **Integrations:** installed integrations and API connections.
- **Library Administration:** import, export, enabled modules, storage, trash,
  backup, and restore.
- **Operations:** background work, health, diagnostics, and updates.
- **Appearance:** preferences and branding.

The top menu bar provides seven stable menus: File, Research, Data, Analyse,
Collaborate, Platform, and Help. Menus provide shortcuts; the navigation tree
is the canonical screen hierarchy.

Hidden library or Science modules also hide their corresponding leaf screens.
Empty navigation branches collapse automatically without deleting retained
data.
