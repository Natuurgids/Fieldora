# Dossiers

Dossiers organize governed records for review, ownership, and decision history. They can be associated with projects, nested under parent dossiers, and assigned to owners and reviewers.

## Manage a dossier

Use the dossier workspace to browse the hierarchy and inspect review history. When creating or updating a dossier, choose its type, project, optional parent, owner, reviewer, and review state. Add a review remark when the decision needs supporting context.

## Review safely

Dossier visibility follows your current capability scope. Review history is part of the governed record, so keep remarks factual and avoid entering secrets that do not belong in the dossier itself.
