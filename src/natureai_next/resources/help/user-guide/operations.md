# Operations

Operations manages governed operational assets, locations, drawings, maintenance records, and calibrations. Use the workspace to inspect operational records and their relationship to projects and facilities.

## Work with operational records

Select the appropriate operations domain, review existing records, and open an item for its details. When creating a record, provide the relevant project or parent relationship, type, status, and descriptive information. Links to related projects open the governed project workspace when your access permits it.

## Governance and safety

Operations is capability-filtered administration content. Record factual operational state and maintenance information, and do not treat visibility of an asset or location as permission to perform a physical or administrative action outside your assigned role.
