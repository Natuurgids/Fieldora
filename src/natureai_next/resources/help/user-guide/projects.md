# Projects

Use Projects to plan and track governed field and research work. The workspace presents projects, phases, tasks, sprints, ownership, status, workload, budget, and schedule views under the access scope granted to you.

## Working with projects

Create or open a project before adding phases and tasks. Use the portfolio views to switch between hierarchy, board, grid, timeline, workload, and budget perspectives without changing the underlying records. Project links open the detailed research workspace for the selected project.

## Access and governance

Projects are capability-filtered. If a project or project action is not visible, your current identity or project role does not grant that capability. Do not use alternate URLs to bypass unavailable controls; deep links are evaluated under the same policy boundary.
